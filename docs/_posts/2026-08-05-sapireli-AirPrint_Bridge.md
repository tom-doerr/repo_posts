# [sapireli/AirPrint_Bridge](https://github.com/sapireli/AirPrint_Bridge)

AirPrint Bridge enables wireless printing from iOS devices to standard network printers on macOS. The script adds AirPrint functionality to printers that do not natively support it, allowing iPhones and iPads to print directly to non-AirPrint hardware over the network.
