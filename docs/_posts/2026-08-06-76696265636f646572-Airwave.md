# [76696265636f646572/Airwave](https://github.com/76696265636f646572/Airwave)

Airwave lets you turn any YouTube, SoundCloud, or Spotify playlist link into a single live stream where everyone hears the exact same audio at the same time. It is self-hosted, requires no accounts or premium APIs, and supports browsers and Sonos.
