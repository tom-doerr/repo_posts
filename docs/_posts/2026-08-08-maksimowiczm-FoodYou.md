# [maksimowiczm/FoodYou](https://github.com/maksimowiczm/FoodYou)

Food You is a free, open-source nutrition tracker that stores all dietary information locally on the device without requiring user accounts. It integrates multiple food databases, including Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central, to enable detailed tracking of macronutrients and micronutrients. The application offers modular home screens for habit customization and supports the creation of personal recipes with immediate nutritional analysis.
