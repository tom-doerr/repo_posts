# [tuanngocptn/nport](https://github.com/tuanngocptn/nport)

NPort is a lightweight ngrok alternative that creates secure HTTP/HTTPS tunnels from your localhost to public URLs using Cloudflare. No configuration, no accounts, just instant tunnels with custom subdomains.
