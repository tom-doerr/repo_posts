# [EgalitarianMonkey/hometube](https://github.com/EgalitarianMonkey/hometube)

HomeTube is a self-hosted downloader that retrieves high-quality videos and playlists from over 1800 sites and automatically organizes them into structured local folders for media servers like Plex and Jellyfin.
